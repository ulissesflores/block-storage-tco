Overview

The storage layer your enterprise workloads actually deserve

IBM Cloud® Object Storage is a highly scalable, resilient service integrated with the IBM Cloud ecosystem. It provides enterprise-level security with built-in encryption and supports compliance, data availability, and multi-zone resiliency for modern data-driven applications. It delivers high performance with fast data transfer options, and is optimized for AI, data lakehouses, cloud-native apps, media, backup, and archives.

  • Store virtually unlimited amounts of data, expanding and contracting at will, paying only for what you use.
  • Flexible storage tiers help to optimize your costs, all with 14 9's (99.999999999999%) of data durability.
  • Select the resiliency option ideal for your needs, including immediately consistent data protection across regions.
  • Role based policies and access permissions help secure data access, with leading encryption key management.
